# Kaqu — Delete your account and data

[Azərbaycan dilində](account-deletion-az.html) · English

This page explains how to delete your Kaqu account and the data linked to it.

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open Kaqu and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile → Delete account** and confirm.

This permanently removes your profile, your pets, your listings, your messages, your bookings and reviews, your favourites and the photos you uploaded. It cannot be undone.

## If you cannot open the app

Write from the email address of your account to **tshukurbeyli@gmail.com** with the subject **"Delete my account"**. We will delete the account and its data within **30 days** and confirm by email.

## What we may keep

Routine backups of our hosting provider are overwritten on a rolling basis. Records we must keep by law, and moderation records about reports and blocks (needed to protect other users), are kept only as long as required.
