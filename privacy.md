# Tuklu — Privacy Policy

[Azərbaycan dilində](privacy-az.html) · English

Last updated: 7 October 2026

## 1. Who we are

Tuklu is operated by **Turan Shukurbayli** (an individual developer), the operator of the personal data described below. Contact for privacy questions and requests: **tshukurbeyli@gmail.com**.
Tuklu is a community app for pet owners in Azerbaijan: finding mates for pets, pet sitters and hotels, veterinarians, and adoption / sale listings.

## 2. What we collect

| Data | Why | Where it is visible |
|---|---|---|
| Email address (to sign in with a one-time code, or provided by Google / Apple when you use them to sign in) | Account, security | Only to you and us |
| Name, profile photo, city (optional) | Your profile, chats, listings | Other users you interact with / public listings |
| Pets you add (name, species, breed, birth date, weight, colour, health flags, about, photos) | Your pet list, mate finder, adoption / sale listings | Private by default; public only if you list the pet for mating, adoption or sale |
| Listings (price, location label, seller type) | Adoption / sale | Public after moderation |
| Messages you send in chats | Communication between users | Participants of that conversation |
| Bookings and reviews (provider, time, status, rating, text) | Appointments and trust | Booking: you and the provider; reviews: public |
| Favourites, likes, matches | Saved items, mate matching | Private to you (a match is shown to both pets' owners) |
| Reports and blocks you make | Safety and moderation | Our moderators only |
| Photos you choose from your library | Pet / profile pictures | As above |
| Push notification token (a device identifier issued by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging; only if you allow notifications) | Delivering message / booking notifications | Only to us and our push provider |
| Search history (your recent searches in the app) | Quick access to your own recent searches | Private to you |
| Approximate location (when you open the map or tap "Use my location" and grant permission) | Distances to clinics and sitters, centring the map | Used on your device only: it is not sent to our servers, not stored and not shown to others |

We do not collect analytics or crash reports, we do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising. Payments are not processed in the app.

## 3. Your consent

Under the Law of the Republic of Azerbaijan "On Personal Data", we collect and process your personal data, and transfer it abroad, with your consent. By creating an account and signing in you confirm that you have read this policy and the Terms of Use and **consent** to:

- the collection and processing of the data listed in section 2 for the purposes stated there;
- the transfer of that data to the service providers in section 4, who are located outside Azerbaijan (Singapore, the United States);
- the processing for as long as your account exists. You can **withdraw your consent at any time** by deleting your account (Profile → Delete account) or by writing to us; we then destroy your data as described in section 6.

You confirm that you are at least 18 years old.

## 4. How it is processed and who receives it

Data is stored with our backend provider **Supabase** (database, authentication, file storage) in the region **Southeast Asia (Singapore)**. Access to data is limited by database row-level security: you can read and change your own data; other users only see what is intended to be public (see the table above). Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

We share data only with: other users (as described above); service providers acting for us — Supabase (data, authentication, files), an email delivery service (one-time sign-in codes), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications; United States), and Google or Apple when you choose to sign in with them; and authorities when legally required. We do not sell or rent data and we do not use data brokers.

## 5. Your rights

You have the right to know whether we hold data about you, on what basis, and who has access to it; to see the data we hold; to have it corrected, archived or destroyed; to object to processing that is not mandatory; and to withdraw your consent. Write to **tshukurbeyli@gmail.com** and we will answer within 30 days. You may also complain to the competent authority — in Azerbaijan, the **National Cybersecurity Agency** — or to a court. See also: [how to delete your account](account-deletion.html).

## 6. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account (**Profile → Delete account**) we remove your profile, pets, listings, messages, bookings, reviews and uploaded photos. Routine backups of our hosting provider are overwritten on a rolling basis. Records we must keep by law, and moderation records about reports and blocks (needed to protect other users), are kept only as long as required.

## 7. Age

Tuklu is for people aged **18 and over** and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you believe a child has used the app, write to us and we will delete the account.

## 8. User content and safety

You can report content and block users in the app. We review reports and pending listings within 24 hours and may remove content or suspend accounts that break the community rules (spam, scams, abuse, illegal animal trade, animal cruelty).

## 9. Security

We take organisational and technical measures to protect personal data from unlawful access, loss or alteration (access control, encryption in transit, restricted database access).

## 10. Changes

We will post changes on this page and, for material ones, notify you in the app and ask for your consent again where the law requires it.
