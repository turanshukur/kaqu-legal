# Kaqu — Terms of Use

[Azərbaycan dilində](terms-az.html) · English

Last updated: 7 October 2026

## 1. The service

Kaqu is a community app for pet owners in Azerbaijan: finding mates for pets, pet sitters and hotels, veterinarians, and adoption / sale listings.
It connects people; the services, animals and prices are offered by the users and providers themselves. The operator, Turan Shukurbayli, is not a party to agreements between users and providers and does not process payments in the app.

## 2. Your account

You must be at least **18** years old. Give correct information and keep your sign-in access private. You can delete your account at any time in the app (**Profile → Delete account**). How we handle your data is described in the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

## 3. Rules

You may not post spam, scams, abusive or illegal content, content that shows or promotes animal cruelty, or listings of animals you are not allowed to give away or sell. We may remove content, reject listings and suspend accounts that break these rules. You can report content and block users in the app; we aim to review reports within 24 hours.

## 4. Animals: sellers' responsibility

If you list an animal, you are responsible for complying with the laws of the Republic of Azerbaijan that apply to keeping, identifying, registering, vaccinating, selling and transporting animals, and for having the required documents. Do not list stray or wild-caught animals or species that may not be traded. We cannot check these documents.

## 5. Listings and bookings

Listings are reviewed before they appear, but we cannot verify every detail. Bookings are requests that the provider confirms; payment is agreed and made directly with the provider. Check animals, providers and documents yourself before you commit.

## 6. Your content

You keep the rights to what you post and allow us to store and show it inside the app as needed to run the service.

## 7. Liability

The app is provided "as is". To the extent the law allows, the operator is not liable for losses arising from agreements between users and providers, from the condition of animals, or from content posted by users. Nothing in these terms limits rights that you have under mandatory law.

We may update these terms and will show material changes in the app. Contact: **tshukurbeyli@gmail.com**. Governing law: the laws of the Republic of Azerbaijan.
